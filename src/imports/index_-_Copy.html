Who We Are

Built on chemistry.
Grown on trust.

Technocom began as the authorized agent for Huntsman in Egypt, one of the world's leading chemical companies. Over the years we evolved into a full polyurethane system house, blending our own custom PU systems alongside a growing technical-commercial agency business.

Today Technocom serves Egypt's industrial, construction, and refrigeration markets with locally blended, globally sourced polyurethane solutions, backed by deep formulation know-how and a broad network of world-class raw material suppliers.

01

Huntsman Agency

Started as Huntsman's authorized agent in Egypt, distributing polyurethane raw materials and building deep market knowledge.

02

System House

Expanded into blending our own PU systems in-house, using recipes and Huntsman know-how developed over years of technical experience in the field.

03

Broader Supplier Network

Grew our supplier partnerships to encompass the world's leading polyurethane raw material producers, offering clients access to the best chemistry at competitive pricing.

04

Polyurea and Beyond

Now launching polyurea coating systems, extending our technical capability into waterproofing, protective coatings, and industrial surface applications.

PU Systems

Rigid polyurethane systems for every application

We supply polyols and MDI systems tailored to the exact requirements of each application, formulated from world-class raw materials and backed by years of in-house technical know-how.

01

Refrigeration

High-performance rigid foam systems for commercial and industrial refrigeration insulation.

02

Water Heaters

Thermal insulation systems for domestic and industrial water heaters, our core and longest-standing production strength.

03

Spray Foam

Two-component spray systems for on-site insulation of roofs, walls, and irregular building surfaces.

04

Pipes and Tanks

Injection systems for pre-insulated pipe networks, underground pipelines, and industrial storage tanks.

05

Sandwich Panels

Continuous and discontinuous panel systems for cold rooms, industrial buildings, and building facades.

06

Foam Blocks

Free-rise block foam systems for fabricators and downstream converters requiring cut-to-size insulation material.

07

Thermoses and Coolers

Precision foam formulations for insulated flask, thermos, and cooler box production at scale.

08

Decoration

Rigid foam systems for architectural decoration, ornamental moulding, and lightweight decorative elements.

Now Launching

Polyurea Systems

The next generation of protective coatings

Technocom is launching polyurea coating systems, an elastomeric, seamless, ultra-fast-cure coating technology for waterproofing, protection, and industrial surface applications across Egypt.

Ultra-fast cure

Polyurea cures in seconds and is fully walkable within minutes, with no dependence on weather conditions or extended downtime.

Superior protection

Exceptional abrasion resistance, chemical resistance, and elasticity across a wide temperature range, outperforming traditional coating systems.

Seamless membrane

Applied as a continuous membrane with no joints or seams, conforming precisely to any surface geometry and eliminating weak points.

01

Roof Waterproofing

Seamless waterproof membrane for rooftops, terraces, balconies, and structural surfaces exposed to weather.

02

Water Tanks and Reservoirs

Protective lining for potable and industrial water storage tanks, resistant to chemicals and abrasion.

03

Industrial Flooring

High-build protective floor coating for warehouses, food processing plants, and heavy-traffic industrial areas.

04

Pipe Coating

External and internal corrosion protection for steel, concrete, and ductile iron pipes in demanding environments.

05

Swimming Pools

Flexible, waterproof pool lining applied rapidly and cured quickly for residential and commercial pool projects.

06

For Spray Applicators

Contractors already operating plural-component spray machines can apply polyurea with the same equipment class, making it a natural extension of existing spray operations.
